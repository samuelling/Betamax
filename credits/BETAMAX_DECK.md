# Betamax deck sounds

The sound of the tape going in (slide, clunk, loading clatter, the threading motor, the PLAY engage) is an edit of two recordings by Daniel D. Teoli Jr., Internet Archive, marked Public Domain (Creative Commons Public Domain Mark 1.0):

- "Sony Betamax Tape Insertion & Ejection Sounds" — https://archive.org/details/sony-beta-tape-insertion-ejection-d.-d.-teoli-jr.
- "Sony Betamax Operating Sounds" — https://archive.org/details/sony-betamax-operating-sounds-d.-d.-teoli-jr.

The running-transport loop is not a sample: it is noise with the measured 1/12-octave spectrum of the PLAY section of the second recording plus its measured tonal lines (head drum and capstan harmonics), fitted to within about 1 dB per third-octave band.
